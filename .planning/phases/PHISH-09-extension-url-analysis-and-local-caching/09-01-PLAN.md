  <action>
    Create extension/src/detection/heuristics.ts implementing HeuristicEngine.
    - Calculate Shannon entropy for URL, host, and path.

  </action>

    - Detects lookalike domains (e.g. paypa1.com, micros0ft-verify.xyz)

  </acceptance_criteria>
</task>
